our little story

01

The name says it all

Guftugu means heartfelt, unhurried conversation. That's exactly what we serve — a warm corner to sit, sip and talk for hours.

02

Made for students & dreamers

Near Amity University, our tables fill with study sessions, first dates and long catch-ups. Everyone finds their nook here.

03

Kind on the pocket

Great taste shouldn't cost a fortune. At ₹1–200 per person, every cup and plate is honest, fresh and generous.
