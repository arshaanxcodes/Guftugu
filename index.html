"Guftugu" — گفتگو / गुफ़्तगू — means conversation. Not small talk, the real kind: unhurried, over a second cup, with someone worth listening to.

Tucked opposite AK Palace Inn near Amity University, this is the kind of cafe regulars quietly claim as their own — a table by the window, the same order of masala chai, a couple of hours that disappear without anyone checking the time.

🐾 Two resident cats consider the place theirs too — don't be surprised if one naps on the chair next to you.
